She made room
before the world did.

There is a particular kind of courage in being first: the first to ask, to leave, to build a life with a wider horizon. And then to turn around and hold the door.

Some legacies arrive without a grand entrance. A meal remembered. A hand on a shoulder. The patience to teach a skill, then the generosity to let someone make it their own.
