ABOUT RE:TUNE

Every special day
has a story.

Re:Tune is a podcast dedicated to discovering the stories behind the special days we celebrate.

From ancient traditions and folklore to modern celebrations, we explore the knowledge and cultural meaning behind them.

Our mission is simple: bring people together through culture, curiosity, and shared stories.

TRADITIONS · TOGETHERNESS · HOPE

Mid-Autumn
Festival

A time to gather beneath the moon, share stories, enjoy mooncakes, and celebrate togetherness.

01
THE STORY

More than a festival beneath the moon.

The Mid-Autumn Festival is a traditional celebration centered around the full moon. Across generations, families gather together, enjoy mooncakes, carry lanterns, and share stories.

At its heart, the festival represents reunion, gratitude, and the connection between people.

STRENGTH · GRACE · INSPIRATION

Women's
Day

A celebration of the women who inspire us, shape our lives, and influence the world around us.

02
THE STORY

A celebration of strength and inspiration.

International Women's Day is a day to recognize women's achievements and reflect on the social, cultural, and historical experiences that shaped the celebration.

Re:Tune explores how different cultures express appreciation, respect, and recognition for women.

GRATITUDE · KNOWLEDGE · INSPIRATION

Teacher's
Day

A day to appreciate the people who teach, guide, and inspire us to discover what we are capable of.

03
THE STORY

Those who teach inspire generations.

Teacher's Day is celebrated in many parts of the world as a way of recognizing the important role educators play in society.

Different countries have developed their own traditions, dates, and ways of expressing gratitude toward teachers.
